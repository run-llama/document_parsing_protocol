# Publication provenance for sample images

The 302 packaged convention images were matched byte-for-byte to the annotation guide’s
cleared publication manifest: 278 constructed images and 24 U.S. federal government images.
`publication-provenance.json` records each local path, SHA-256, source kind, clearance decision,
and attribution. Convention pages display credits for the federal sources they use.

Run `python3 scripts/audit-sample-provenance.py` to refresh this
record. The sync script runs this check too. It rejects uncleared sources, missing attribution,
changed image bytes, and unreviewed files in the packaged convention-image directory.
This checks recorded provenance and asset identity, not a fresh legal determination.

Additional teaching images are original constructed SVGs, generated by
`scripts/build-teaching-examples.py`, with no customer documents or third-party assets.
They are labeled constructed and dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0.
Their expected encodings are authored teaching examples, not provider outputs or score data.
The separate IRS and Apple public source documents are credited in README-documents.md.

All sample routes and assets remain behind the existing site password.
