# Ground Truth — Inline Formatting

What to do, when producing ground truth, in the cases the inline-formatting rules leave to review.
Each step serves the rule it links to; the rule decides the output.

| Situation | Step | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A drop-cap initial is genuinely unreadable | Flag the page for review; do not guess the letter | [[Drop Caps]] |
| A line-end hyphen cannot be classed as a break or part of the word | Keep the hyphen, join the parts, and flag the word | [[Line-End Hyphenation]] |
| Link anchor text differs from its target, and the target looks like it carries real meaning | Keep the printed words and flag the page | [[Links]] |
| An inset block is a real quotation and its quotation status looks load-bearing | Flatten it as usual and flag the page | [[Quote Blocks]] |
| A page carries verse or other text whose line breaks are content | Apply the working default and flag the page, so it can be revisited when the open point closes | [[Verse and Hard Line Breaks]] |
